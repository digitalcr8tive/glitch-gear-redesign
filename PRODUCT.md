# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Glitch Gear shoppers, gaming fans, collectors, and prospective customers evaluating licensed gaming apparel. Visitors need to browse recognizable franchises, compare products, understand sizing and policies, build a cart, and feel confident that the storefront is credible before checkout.

## Product Purpose

Create a complete demonstration storefront for Glitch Gear that presents the company's current catalog, product imagery, sizing guidance, and store information through a new visual system based directly on the supplied design. The demonstration should make the redesign tangible to the business while keeping the eventual Shopify checkout integration clearly deferred.

## Brand Personality

Immersive, battle-worn, and confident. The interface should feel like a premium game loadout screen translated into a fashion storefront, with recognizable franchises and products taking priority over decorative effects.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic Shopify templates, bright lifestyle-fashion minimalism, soft SaaS styling, cartoonish gamer motifs, and decorative effects that reduce product readability. Do not invent products, prices, sizing claims, policies, testimonials, or licensing relationships that are not supported by the current Glitch Gear website.

## Design Principles

1. Treat every product as collectible gear from a recognizable world.
2. Keep shopping actions obvious even when the presentation is cinematic.
3. Use the current Glitch Gear website as the source of truth for catalog and policy content.
4. Make the complete shopping journey demonstrable without implying that live payment processing is already connected.
5. Preserve the supplied visual concept across desktop and mobile rather than simplifying it into a generic storefront.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain readable contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, semantic structure, useful image alternative text, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts. Avoid rapid flashing and ensure that neon accent colors are never the sole carrier of meaning.
